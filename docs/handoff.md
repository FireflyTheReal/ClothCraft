**15 October pass (CLO3D analysis and assembled nodal operator).** REA/Ghidra on CLO 2026.1.310: the implicit operator of `libCloSimulateSolvers.dylib` is a **mixed block-CSR** with symmetric 3x3 nodal blocks (diagonal over six floats, off-diagonal over nine), solved by conjugate gradient preconditioned by the **inverted nodal diagonal**. ClothCraft now adopts this assembled operator in the Newton reference: `src/blocksolve/` (`SymMat3`, `MatMixCsr`, `block_jacobi_pcg`) and `System::assemble_operator` / `System::pcg`. The matrix-free point-Jacobi path remains the fallback (`System::pcg_point_jacobi`) when a nodal block is not invertible. Guards: `blocksolve_assembly_matches_the_matrix_free_operator` (assembly must reproduce `apply` to within 1e-9, including fixed dofs) and `block_jacobi_bounds_point_jacobi_iterations`. Measurement: 52 versus 64 iterations on `two_panel_demo`, but **no gain** on the T-shirt fixtures, whose nodal blocks are almost diagonal (ratio 0.025 versus 0.24). No time gain is claimed. Artifact identities, Evidence IDs, measurements and corrections from the previous pass: [clo3d-reverse-engineering.md](clo3d-reverse-engineering.md).

## First publication and editing

Read the [AI entry point](../AI_START_HERE.md), the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md), the [editing guide](authoring.md) and the [manual study](manual-study.md). The editor now offers a single 2D board: Bézier pen, rectangle, editable points/handles, piece layout, edge sewing from 2D or 3D, then gizmo placement. `board.rs` carries the 2D interactions, `edges_3d.rs` the visible 3D selection, `garment/curves.rs` the curve subdivision. The CPU commands validate the document before replacing it; the worker ignores old generations. The industry formats and the common backend contract remain to be built.

Read first [the false-elasticity correction and the seam removal](adaptive-initialization.md). The [fabrics and performance](perf-fabrics-validation.md) validation predates this physical correction and used eight iterations; the default is now 16. The [stationary-panel correction](apron-validation.md), the [board validation](board-validation.md) and that of the [first publication](publication-validation.md) remain historical. `serde_json/float_roundtrip` is required to recover exactly the same mesh after saving. A 2D layout changes neither the rest UVs nor the 3D state. Physical edits rebuild the caches, but `Edit::state_after` preserves positions/velocities of panels that are compatible by ID and local topology, including on a mass change; only new, remeshed or moved panels restart from the arrangement.

The conservative inertial prediction was missing on the GPU: a free rectangle fell far too slowly. Adding a prediction with whole gravity then revealed a false-elasticity effect under a limited budget. **Do not return to either of these two versions**: `vbd/prediction.rs` now applies the VBD §3.7 adaptive initialization with damping correction, still within the OGC bounds. The inertial target keeps all of gravity. The opt-in GPU tests check the analytic fall, topology growth and five seconds of hanging cloth. The two pink edges remain a preview: **Sew and simulate** commits this relation before starting the worker. The seam list is available in every mode; selecting a sewn edge exposes **Delete the seam**, or **Del**, with Ctrl+Z and preservation of the drape.

**Quality and performance of the previous pass:** 76 CPU tests, two opt-in GPU tests and 37 native actions pass. On a new 25 mm T-shirt, after four seconds, the old eight-iteration version left 362.51 mm of seam gap and 51.61% P95 extension. Adaptive initialization alone reduces these values to 2.18 mm / 8.35%; the new default of 16 gives 2.61 mm / 4.79%. After ten seconds: 2.19 mm / 4.48%, then about 60 frames/s and 60 physical steps/s on an RTX 3090. The apron remains at 52.36 steps/s (0.873×) and keeps significant local deformations: its real-time threshold and its final quality are not established. Read [protocol, measurements and limitations](adaptive-initialization.md). The BVH traversal gains in [the previous pass](perf-fabrics-validation.md) remain historical. CPU detection remains dominant; preserve the free-fall **and** supported-cloth regression tests.

**Fabrics:** `src/fabrics.rs` provides nine presets and the conversion between extension under 5 N / 5 cm ↔ membrane coefficient. `authoring::Command::Fabric` performs the transactional assignment; `atelier/fabrics_ui.rs` exposes area weight, garment mass, warp/weft stretch, thickness and stiffnesses. Changing a single piece detaches the shared material. The mechanical values are estimated; only the referenced masses/thicknesses come from samples or supplier datasheets. Read [the sources and limitations](fabrics.md), then [the performance research](performance-research.md). AVBD and the Fabric-101 models are not implemented.

Next editing tasks: numeric dimensions, holes/internal lines, partial seams, multi-selection, portable save and feedback on initial intersections. See the limitations and gestures in [authoring.md](authoring.md).

## Turbo pass

Read [the historical measurements and performance contracts](turbo-perf.md). Detection remains f64 CPU: point/triangle/edge BVH with fixed and moving roots, traversal by node pairs, moving refit, conservative candidate cache and reduction over two tasks. `contact_profile` distinguishes refit, candidates and exact detection; `detection_ms` also includes coloring on the hybrid GPU backend. Forces/radii remain recomputed at every step. The GPU uses a workgroup of 32 threads per vertex, reusable allocations and a cache of material terms. The atelier has a worker clocked independently of rendering, a single pending result and a `--ui-perf-smoke` test on the real window. `scripts/turbo-perf.ps1 -UiPerf` reproduces the trials.

## Current path

- `cloth-fit` now uses VBD + OGC by default. Without `--gpu-sim`, the CLI remains f64 CPU; the atelier uses the hybrid GPU backend on a worker and keeps the interface responsive.
- `--reference` keeps Newton/PCG and its old vertex/avatar contact. The results in `first-fit.md` and `first-fit-results.json` are historical, before OGC and before the widening of the patterns.
- `ogc/` implements admissible regions, reciprocal VF, EE, self-contact, quadratic/logarithmic activation and conservative bounds. `vbd.rs` uses the same FEM/seam energies as the reference.
- `gpu_vbd.rs` + `vbd.wgsl` compute the VBD forces and updates on GPU. Detection, coloring and packing remain CPU. No AVBD, PD, IPC or MAS backend.
- `atelier.rs`: 3D view on the left, unified 2D board and contextual inspector on the right; examples in the top menu; grab a triangle by its barycentrics with the left button, orbit with the right, pan with the middle. Space: play/pause. Attachment is an energy, not a forced displacement that would ignore contacts.
- `render.rs`: avatar resident on GPU, camera matrix applied in WGSL, interpolated normals. Rendering and simulation have distinct measurements.
- The T-shirt/dress patterns have been widened. Their initial arrangement consists of separate planes in front of/behind the avatar, without the per-vertex warp that could create intersections between vertices. Old intersected scenes are rejected by OGC; rebuild an arrangement.
- V1/v2 templates, 2D rest state in metres, per-panel oriented materials, constrained Delaunay and exports are preserved. CC0 MakeHuman avatar of 1.75 m / 26,756 triangles.

## Remaining priorities

1. Port VF/EE detection, BVH refit and list construction to GPU, with tests against f64 distances and explicit handling of any overflow. Candidate rebuilds remain the main source of spikes, even after the conservative cache. Do not announce a fully GPU solver.
2. Allocations and material caches are now reused, and cooperative groups fix the low occupancy. It remains to avoid full transfers per step, then to validate dense and animated scenes. The `gpu_compute_ms` timestamps exclude CPU and transfers.
3. The isotropic bounds slow sliding; the hybrid backend renews them once per step. Study GPU renewal and directional methods separately, without claiming that they are already present.
4. Seams remain finite penalties. The exclusions are local to the sewn correspondences; no guarantee for those pairs. Add a sewn topology or an augmented coupling to close exactly without degenerating the triangles.
5. Friction, animated avatar, multi-layer thickness and material calibration remain absent. The OGC forces are per pair and their calibration depends on the resolution.
6. Keep the physical tests: finite differences, action/reaction, objectivity, internal C2 continuity, VF/EE crossings, accumulation of bounds, invalid initial state, pins and mouse grabbing. The intersection diagnostic uses floating tolerances, not exact predicates.
